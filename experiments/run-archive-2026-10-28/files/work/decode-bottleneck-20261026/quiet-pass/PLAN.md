These deliberately impossible cached-output interventions estimate recoverable latency under this replay; they are not deployable candidates or mathematical guarantees about all implementations.

Time the native and two frozen candidate expert operators, their isolated up/gate/activation prefixes, and the native up/gate region, raw up/gate products, down projection and bias/SwiGLU alone, using the same real inputs from layers 0/12/23 and eight evenly spread decode steps.
